Auyon Siddiq

Associate Professor of Decisions, Operations and Technology Management

UCLA Anderson School of Management

auyon.siddiq@anderson.ucla.edu

I’m an Associate Professor of Decisions, Operations and Technology Management at the UCLA Anderson School of Management. My research focuses on digital platforms and marketplaces, particularly how pricing, information, and competition shape economic outcomes. I draw on game theory, optimization, and econometrics, sometimes developing new computational methods. My recent work examines AI’s effects on work and decision-making.

I'm teaching a new MBA class called Science and Strategy of Artificial Intelligence. I have also taught core statistics in the full-time MBA program and core optimization in the Master of Science in Business Analytics (MSBA) program. In 2020, I was named to Poets & Quants' list of the Best 40-Under-40 Business School Professors.

I serve as an Associate Editor at Manufacturing & Service Operations Management (M&SOM) and the INFORMS Journal on Data Science (IJDS).

Before joining UCLA, I received a PhD in Operations Research from the University of California, Berkeley.
